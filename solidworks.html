Wooden Designs

Complete

When I got confortable with SolidWorks, everything I made for myself were of assemble-dismantle models. Altough they were metal Study tables, cloth hanger, shelf, and more, I wanted to be able to disassemble them whenever I want if I were to make them. Then around January 2025 I remembered of the living room tables I have back home. I remembered them because there was one that took me two to three hours to assemble and also because of how the wood blanks were connected to each other to give that nail/screw free apperance on the exterior.

Hence I wanted to try that precise wooden design that was facinating to me. So I did. I looked of interesting living room tables online and mentally dismantled them to create the nessecery number of pieces. Then I recreated those models and even added some modifications to it. The result was exciting and I was able to get that screw-free outside look.

Simulations

Complete

One of the key features of SolidWorks is to be able to simulate the design in a digital environment. This help to know the safety, failure likelyhood, and life span for the parts and projects. This analysis help to better design the product and ensure it reliability in real situation.

After saying all that, I am unfortunately not totally confident on my simulation abilities, but I can still do the needy when necessary for I know my way around.

Hand Drawings

Complete

Although this process is not as effective as SolidWorks designing, it help me get a somewhat close mental image aof the complex parts and mechanism. I do it prior designing when I do not have a fix mental depiction of the part/mechanism or when I do not have my machine with me. Drawing them down helps securing the idea and allows me to safety play with the part mentally without the risk out "overwriting"/forget initial concept.

First Designs With SolidWorks

Complete

The very first academic design is made was for group projects. The project was concerning beach cleaning robot and it was in August 2024. That was the semester we learnt SolidWorks and I saw the ease it offers compared to blender when it comes to mechanical design (that is my opinion). So I used SolidWorks and made that red-blue truck looking vehicle with an hand on its forhead. Looking back now, this design just feels funny and was certainly not created for real implementation.

Moving shortly forwards in time, I decided to practice my design skill by designing some few things I wanted in my appartment and even the appartment itself. For the items I wanted in my appartment I designed a kitchen shelf, a cloth hanger and tried my hand on study table - yes tried, because that thing I did not look anything close to a table to even call it study table. As for the appartment digital representation, well it was easy, the rooms were empty, thus I only had to make the layout, fortunately for the newby me.

Those were the ones I remember the story behind, so this is it for this section. The slideshow here will, of course, start with my Logo so you can be situated in the progression.
