KJC 3D Printer

Personal [Mars 2025 - Ongoing]

The Idea here is to build a personal 3d printer that I will be able to tailor and tune to whatever I desire. The main points are it has to have a significant print volume/capacity and customizable enough for me to add any desired features.

The printer will not operate on belt and pulley but rather on lead-screw mechanism. We know belt and pulley offers faster print time and offer less energy demending system. However we are prioritizing safety, the print can take as long as necessary.

The motion guider shafts and the threaded shafts have been chosen after safety calculation. We are trying to prevent failure due to stress and bending, as well as buckling. The selected diameters are all above the minimum diameters result obtained from the greatest failure criteria owing to the position and arrangement of the shafts. Moreover the motors are also selected after manually calculating the motor requirement. They will operates on ramps and an arduino mega for the prototype and the boards will be upated after the syten is proven to operate successfully.

WeUp UX/UI Internship

Professional [2021-2023]

Although this internship was Grphic Design related, it thought me immensely on how to visualize flow and plan multiple path mentally throught the Figma excecution flow demo. However before we even got to the flow demo, the character and accessories vectorization added to the meetings painted and spotlighted the significant influence of small steps.

As I was saying, in begining, with WeUp we had several online meetings where we were getting acquainted with the other members and the main features of the app, I understood that proper planning and accurate scope description are not steps to be taken lightly as they serve of foundation for the rest to come. I mean we took our time and a considerable number of meeting to define and refine those aspect and I am only taking from when i joined. Looking Back, I understand better why we were working everithing possible to get all of us on the same foot.

When everything was set through the initial meetings, it was time to distribute the role, mine initially being character and accessories vectorization. I was under the supervision on M. Zack. He was to draw the characters and landscape component and I, of course, were to make them digitally usable, so we were to bring the app layout from imagination to sketch, then from sketch to digital. We worked in a feedback manner in which when the work is submitted, we received constructive advices on what could be made better. From this single process I saw the huge impact minor changes and strive to consistantly put this lesson to use in my life.

Once the charaters and the accessories were done, we proceeded on the app layout and function. For the sake of the vision I got to know of figma and immediately applied this knewly acquired skill to use. Thus it was the begining of my UX/UI journey. Create flow, test flow, debug flow, and improve flow was now my day to the point were I can still see the interaction arrows while I am writing this. This part of the intership taught me that everything complex started simple (2 artboards conneted to +200 connections). Yet when it was time for debugging the route was clear. Hence the final lesson: doing is important, but doing with knowing is clarity.

The whole WeUp experience put to light a fact that I was not aware of about myself. When it comes to me, I know the number of straight days I can work with no sleep, which I would keep for now. Moreover I have a well defined environment where I do so, however for that internship I was practically working everywhere I had my laptop and identified something that was to be modified.

Robotics Club Projects - SoluIoTech at OpenLabs,Accra

Professional [2021-2022]

SoluIoTech was a group of lively and passionate individuals looking for ways to apply their knowledge beyong classroom. Led by a great man named Loveway, they aimed to find new and up-to-date solutions to problems using IoT. I joined the group while i was doing my certification in Robotics and Cyber Security at OpenLabs. I found myself having the role of the programmer due to my familiarity and understanding of C++, Python, Java, and Kotlin. Moreover I was a good fit for that rule thanks to my fast learning and adaptability, and puzzle-like knowledge (ability to combine knowledge from different backgrounds to built). Yet whenever things were difficult and Help was needed I could always count on our Leader Loveway, Asante, as well as other members, to give me an hand with the coding aspect. The connections were made by Miss Flory and Sandra - very nice and engaged ladies. We were a group of +7 people.

We worked on several projects throughout my stay, notably: * A nose mask detector that was to scan the people entering the building using openCV and machine learning on python (the project was inspired by the covid era). * A smart door that opens up when only to the house owner using facial verification from a model similar to the smart door. * A smart home that was using the smart door system at the entrance, keeping track on the temperature and the number of person in every single room to switch the lights and fans on/off. * A blind stick and glass assit which was a proposed project to help the neighbouring blind association. * A smart farming system that was to monitor the pH,and humidity of the soil, and automatically resolve the identified offence to the plant. We also implemented random noise emission to scare the pests.

Those are some of the projects we had the chance to work on and to which i could contribute. A lot of laughter, joy, and frustation were shared throughout those projects and at the Robotics Exposition in 2022. I am genuinely glad to have worked with such people for I have learn a lot from them.
